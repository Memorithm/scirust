## Interpretation and continuation

The observations demonstrate sensitivity to sampling and input/readout placement. They do not measure neural computation. In particular, weak connectivity and absence of isolated nodes do not guarantee a directed path from a selected input to a selected readout. A later recurrent experiment must retain this boundary rather than silently moving ports or selecting favorable seeds.

The edge-count-only control changes the number of isolated nodes in the rank-only cases (437, 377 and 363 versus reference 923, 892 and 930). Degree-preserving arms retain those reference counts. Hence an edge-count-only comparator is insufficient to attribute a future effect specifically to detailed V888 topology.

Invariant preservation and changed edges do not establish uniform sampling, chain mixing, independent null ensembles, preserved SCCs or higher motifs. The bounded switch-chain budget is not a convergence proof. Anatomical block matching is not functional modularity matching.

Next sub-slice **V888-BOOL-0.2b** must characterize control diversity/mixing diagnostics, anatomical versus structural partition choice, and fixed-port reachability/placement sensitivity before topology-effect attribution. Existing pilot observations stay frozen. Parent 0.2 remains active; downstream dynamics and delayed XOR are not declared completed.
