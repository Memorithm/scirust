## Question

Phase 0 established that total lineage morphology/connectivity rises with annotated lineage neuron count. VG-1 asks whether that relationship is uniform across nervous-system regions and which lineages allocate unusually high or low structure after controlling for neuron count.

The fitted descriptive model remains:

`total_metric = a * n_neurons^b`

and residuals are measured in log-response units relative to that fit.

## Regional result

The central brain, optic lobe and ventral nerve cord do **not** share the same scaling pattern.

| Metric | Central brain b | Optic lobe b | VNC b |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cable length | 0.734 | 0.933 | 0.903 |
| volume | 0.680 | 0.948 | 0.905 |
| branchpoints | 0.904 | **1.202** | **1.161** |
| endpoints | 0.858 | **1.139** | **1.116** |
| axon length | 0.687 | 0.920 | 0.910 |
| dendrite length | 0.743 | **1.026** | 0.974 |
| input connections | 0.762 | **1.018** | 0.967 |
| output connections | 0.720 | 0.910 | **1.000** |

Group counts:
- central brain: 207 lineage-region groups;
- optic lobe: 34;
- ventral nerve cord: 60.

The central-brain panel is consistently sublinear. By contrast, optic-lobe and VNC arbor-complexity measures approach or exceed linear scaling with lineage neuron count. The strongest superlinear point estimates are branchpoints and endpoints.

This is the first concrete evidence that a single global "brain growth exponent" would be misleading for this adult connectome.

## Size-adjusted residual examples

Residuals identify lineages whose adult structure is unusually large or small relative to the global lineage-size expectation.

Examples of declared high residuals:
- cable: `ALl1_dorsal`, `VLPl2_lateral`, `27X`, `DM1_posterior`;
- volume: `SMPpv2_ventral`, `27X`, `ALad1__prim`, `ALl1_dorsal`;
- branchpoints: `DM1_posterior`, `LB0_posterior`, `MD0__prim`;
- inputs: `VLPl2_lateral`, `17X`, `ALl1_dorsal`;
- outputs: `ALad1__prim`, `DM1_posterior`, `VLPl2_lateral`.

Examples repeatedly below the global size-adjusted expectation include `SLPpm2` and several `DM*_CX_v` / `DM_CX` groups for morphology.

These are candidate follow-up strata, not biological "high-growth" or "low-growth" labels. Region, cell-type composition, reconstruction completeness and lineage annotation can all contribute to residuals.

## Interpretation

VG-1 strengthens a more specific hypothesis:

> Adult neural architecture is not generated by one uniform scaling rule. The relationship between lineage size and arbor complexity is region-dependent, with central brain showing stronger sublinearity and optic-lobe/VNC arbor complexity approaching or exceeding linearity.

This result narrows the search for growth mechanisms. It suggests that later developmental comparisons should test **region- and lineage-specific allocation rules**, rather than only total neuron production.
